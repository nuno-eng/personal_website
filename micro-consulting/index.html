What Micro-Consulting Is

A sharp, bounded engagement

For businesses that need hands-on operational fixes now, not a twelve-week programme.

Micro-consulting is a short, focused engagement built around one operational problem: a process that keeps breaking, a workflow nobody can explain, a bottleneck that runs through you and only you. We diagnose it, fix it, and hand you a system you can run without me in the room.

Most small and mid-sized businesses don't need a strategy overhaul. They need the day-to-day operation to stop depending on the founder's memory. That's what this is built to fix.

How It Helps SMBs

Less founder dependency

If the business stops when you're on holiday, that's not a system, it's a single point of failure. We document and standardise the workflows that currently exist only in your head.

Faster, cleaner execution

A standardised process is a faster process. We strip out the rework, the duplicated effort, and the decisions that get made three different ways by three different people.

Systems that scale

What works at five people breaks at fifteen. We build the operating structure so growth adds revenue, not chaos.

A clear diagnostic, not a guess

Before anything gets fixed, it gets measured. You'll know exactly what the problem is costing you and why it's happening, in plain terms.

How the engagement runs:

01

Diagnostic

We isolate the specific operational problem and map exactly where it's costing you time, money, or control.

02

Redesign

The workflow gets rebuilt, standardised, and written down, so it doesn't rely on any one person's memory.

03

Handover

You and your team run the new system with a clear standard operating procedure, not a set of vague suggestions.

04

Check

A short follow-up to confirm the fix is holding under real operating conditions, and adjust it if it isn't.
