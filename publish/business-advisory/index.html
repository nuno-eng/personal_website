The Vessel Operating System (VOS)

Three pillars, one system

For agency founders and leadership teams who've had enough of vague advice.

This is built on operational standards, practical tools, and accountability, not pep talks. We work a fixed cadence. Every session ends with a standard to hit before the next one. Progress is measured, not felt.

Every business rests on three pillars. VOS scores each one separately, because a business can be strong in one and weak in another at the same time, and that gap is usually where the risk is hiding.

The Bridge

Vision & Strategy

Where the business is steered from: alignment, strategy, and the plan the whole team is actually rowing towards. A shaky Bridge means people work hard but pull in different directions.

The Hull

Systems & Cash

What keeps the business watertight and moving: the money, the numbers you steer by, and the processes that deliver what you sold. A brilliant Bridge can still take on water here.

The Cargo Hold

Product & Marketing

What you carry to market: the demand you create, what you sell, and the customers who come back. A strong Hull with an empty hold goes nowhere.

How the programme runs:

01

The Vessel Assessment

A structured check across all three pillars, scored honestly, so you know exactly where the risk is before you spend anything fixing it.

02

A 90-day plan, built from your weakest points

Installed in weeks 1 to 4, embedded in weeks 5 to 8, reviewed in weeks 9 to 12. Not a long list of everything that's wrong, the few things worth doing next.

03

An accountability system

Tracks what was committed to and what actually happened.

04

Direct, honest feedback

If something isn't working, you'll hear it before it costs you.
