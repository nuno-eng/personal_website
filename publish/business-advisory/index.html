The Vessel Operating Model

Three pillars, one system

For founders and leadership teams who've had enough of vague advice.

This is built on operational standards, practical tools, and accountability, not pep talks. We work a fixed cadence. Every session ends with a standard to hit before the next one. Progress is measured, not felt.

Every business rests on three pillars. The Vessel Operating Model scores each one separately, because a business can be strong in one and weak in another at the same time, and that gap is usually where the risk is hiding.

The Bridge

Vision & Strategy

Where the business is steered from: alignment, strategy, and the plan the whole team is actually rowing towards. A shaky Bridge means people work hard but pull in different directions.

The Hull

Momentum

What actually moves the business through the water: cash, margin, revenue, and how fast day-to-day operations run. A strong Bridge with a slow Hull still goes nowhere.

The Cargo Hold

Long-Term Value

What the business is actually worth carrying forward: financial visibility, the ability to keep improving, and a team that can run things without you in the room.
