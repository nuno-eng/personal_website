What micro consulting is

A sharp, bounded engagement around one problem.

You know something needs fixing, but neither you nor your team has the time to fix it. A process that keeps breaking, a workflow nobody can explain, a bottleneck that runs through you and only you. As an external partner, micro consulting isolates that one problem, fixes it, and hands your team a documented system they can run without you in the room.

No long programme and no strategy overhaul. Just the day-to-day operation no longer depending on the founder’s memory.

How it runs

Four steps. One clear fix.

01

Diagnostic

Isolate the specific problem and map where it costs you time, money or control.

02

Redesign

Rebuild and standardise the workflow, and write it down.

03

Handover

Your team runs the new system with a clear standard operating procedure.

04

Check

A short follow-up to confirm the fix holds under real conditions.

Which one do you need?

Micro consulting or Business Advisory.

Micro consulting fixes one part of the operation. Business Advisory changes how the whole business is run, so it stops depending on you.
